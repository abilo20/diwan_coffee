ABOUT DIWAN

Fresh taste.
Real local hospitality.

Diwan Coffee — ديوان القهوة — is a beloved gathering spot located in Dessie, Ethiopia. We serve rich Ethiopian coffee, freshly blended juices, and delicious cafe bites. More than just coffee — it's a cafe where friends meet, students study, and families share good moments.

Whether you are stopping by for your morning coffee, a quick lunch, or a relaxing evening juice with friends, we welcome you with warmth and quality service. Check our latest updates on TikTok or navigate straight to us on Google Maps.
